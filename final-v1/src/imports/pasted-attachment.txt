1) Background and Problem Statement
Recollection Wisconsin’s website experience must better support three distinct user intents without overpromising functionality RW doesn’t control (many resource links lead to third-party collection platforms). The existing prototype primarily addresses the Contributor flow, which RW fully controls, and must now be expanded to include Educator and Genealogist pathways in a way that aligns with:
* Current RW site structure and constraints (resources are indexed/metadata-based; collections open externally; teacher pages rely on curated pathways rather than deep item-level control)
* Meeting findings:
    * Teachers are time-poor and “bounce” quickly if they don’t get to images and usable resources fast.
    * Online Exhibits and “Main Street Mondays” are strong entry points because they provide immediate visuals + clear framing.
    * Subject-tagged collections can mislead teachers because subject tags apply to collections, not necessarily every item.
    * Map exploration is a “dream feature” but the data exists—wireframe should present it as a proposed/ideal capability, not required to be live now.
    * Genealogists are research-oriented and expect to search by surname + place and accept some friction / no results.
* Kristen’s contributor clarifications (RWDI is not a pathway; hosting/harvesting only; form copy changes; CC0 link; “every three months”; form receipt option)

6) Educator Path (Built from meeting insights + current RW setup)
EDU-1 — Educator Hub: /educators
Primary purpose: get teachers to usable visuals/resources fast, without requiring deep search literacy.
Required sections (wireframe blocks):
1. Hero / Purpose
    * Title: “Teaching Resources”
    * One-sentence framing: “Use primary sources to support Wisconsin history instruction.”
    * CTA buttons:
        * “Explore by Map” → /educators/map
        * “View Online Exhibits” → /educators/exhibits
2. Fast Visual Entry Points (must be above fold)
    * Card: “Online Exhibits (visual + curated)” → /educators/exhibits
    * Card: “Main Street Mondays (series)” → either a stub section or link into exhibits index with a “series” label
    * Card: “Search Starters (canned searches)” → /educators/search-starters
3. Quick Find (optional but aligns with time-poor constraint)
    * CTA: “Find something in 60 seconds” → /educators/quick-find
Copy requirements from meeting:
* Emphasize this is for primary sources and images/maps.
* Avoid promising item-level precision; instead offer curated paths.

EDU-2 — Map Explorer: /educators/map (Map feature belongs here)
Purpose: provide the “ideal” interface teachers want: “find your community” + browse images fast.
Wireframe requirements:
* A Wisconsin map placeholder (simple outline is fine).
* Interaction model (wireframe simulation):
    * Clicking a region/county highlights it.
    * A results panel populates with “Resources from this area” (mock items ok).
* Include a clear note:
    * “Concept / future enhancement: RW has geographic metadata but implementation depends on development.”
Must not imply it exists today on the live site; this is a proposal in the redesign.

EDU-3 — Online Exhibits Index: /educators/exhibits
Purpose: deliver visuals and curated story structure quickly.
Wireframe requirements:
* Exhibit cards showing:
    * Title
    * 1-line description
    * Thumbnail placeholder
    * “Open exhibit” link (can be a stub click that stays in prototype)
Bridge requirement: At top or bottom include:
* “Want more images on this topic?” → link to /educators/search-starters
This directly reflects the meeting idea: exhibits are the best “image-first” entry.

EDU-4 — Search Starters: /educators/search-starters
Purpose: provide “canned searches” as a practical bridge into a huge collection when teacher intent is broad.
Wireframe requirements:
* Two sections:
    1. Search by Place (town/city/county examples)
    2. Search by Topic/Era (statehood, logging, civil rights in WI, etc.)
* Each starter should be a clickable link style row (even if it doesn’t actually call DPLA in wireframe)
* Include microcopy:
    * “These links help you jump into the larger collection without needing to browse full collections.”
This is a direct response to the teacher pain point described in the meeting.

EDU-5 — Quick Find Wizard: /educators/quick-find (recommended)
Purpose: respect time-poor teachers: one quick step to get 3 recommended starting points.
Wireframe requirements:
* 3-step selector:
    * grade band
    * time available
    * topic (optional)
* Output: “3 recommended resources”
    * at least one “Exhibit”
    * at least one “Map exploration suggestion”
    * at least one “Search starter pack”

7) Genealogist Path (Built from meeting insights + site constraints)
GEN-1 — Genealogist Hub: /genealogists
Purpose: set expectations and give immediate search capability.
Wireframe requirements:
* Search bar with helper text:
    * “Try: last name + place (e.g., Coffin Platteville)”
* CTA: “Search” → /genealogists/search
* Tips block:
    * Use surname + town/county
    * Try alternate spellings
    * No results is common; try fewer terms

GEN-2 — Search Screen: /genealogists/search
Wireframe requirements:
* Search input
* Optional fields:
    * place
    * year range (optional)
* Results state:
    * list of “items” with title/location/date/type
* No-results state:
    * “No results found”
    * suggestions (remove one term, try county instead of city, etc.)
This aligns with the meeting reality: genealogists are search-driven and accept iteration.
